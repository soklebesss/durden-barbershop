# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Neighborhood clients of a street-style barbershop — skaters, suits, students, dads. They visit on a phone between things, want to see prices fast, check who's cutting, look at receipts (the lookbook), and book a chair in under a minute. Desktop visitors are browsing the vibe.

## Product Purpose

Single-page marketing site for CUTTHROAT BARBER CLUB (fictional demo, Amsterdam). It exists to convert visits into chair bookings and walk-ins, and to broadcast the shop's attitude. Success: visitor knows the price, the barber, and the address within one scroll, and the "Book a chair" sheet is never more than one tap away.

## Brand Personality

Blunt. Confident. Street. Craft-obsessed without being precious. Manifesto: "STAY SHARP OR STAY HOME." Copy talks like a friend who won't lie to you — short sentences, verdicts, no corporate softening.

## Anti-references

- SaaS template grammar: centered hero + three feature cards + gradient, purple/blue tech gradients, glassmorphism.
- "Gentlemen's grooming lounge" luxury clichés: serif gold-on-black, cucumber-water refinement.
- Chain-salon genericism: stock smiles, "book your experience today!", emoji bullets.
- Uniform AI scaffolding: identical card grids, fade-up-on-everything, eyebrow kickers repeated mechanically.

## Design Principles

1. **Type is the interface.** Oversized display type carries hierarchy; decoration is texture (grain, halftone, tape), never chrome.
2. **One accent, used like a weapon.** Hazard orange marks action, price, danger — nothing else.
3. **Poster wall, not template.** Asymmetry, overlap, off-grid rotation; every section reads like a zine spread.
4. **Loud but legible.** Attitude never costs contrast, focus visibility, or keyboard reach.
5. **Everything books a chair.** Every section offers a path into the booking sheet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: contrast ≥4.5:1 body / ≥3:1 large type, visible focus rings, full keyboard operability (hover reveals must also trigger on focus), skip link, semantic landmarks, labelled forms with inline errors. `prefers-reduced-motion` gets a genuinely calm experience: no marquees, no parallax, fades instead of wipes, no intro stamp.
