## 1. Positioning

**What it is:** A neighborhood barber club that treats a haircut like craft, not customer service. Chairs run by barbers with names, styles, and opinions. You come for the fade, you stay because the room feels like yours.

**Who it's for:** People who care how they walk out the door. Skaters, suits, students, dads — the block, not a demographic.

**Against what:** Strip-mall clip chains and "grooming lounges" with cucumber water. We're the opposite of beige.

**One-line positioning:** *The barbershop your barbershop warns you about.*

**Manifesto:** **STAY SHARP OR STAY HOME.**

Long-form manifesto (usable as hero/footer copy):

> Clippers out. Excuses out.
> We cut heads, not corners.
> No memberships, no upsells, no "grooming journey."
> A chair, a blade, thirty minutes — and you leave better than you came.
> STAY SHARP OR STAY HOME.

## 2. Voice & Tone

### Personality

| Trait | We Are | We Are Not |
|-------|--------|------------|
| Blunt | Short sentences. Verdicts, not pitches. | Rude, edgy-for-edgy's-sake |
| Confident | "Best fade on the block. Come check the receipts." | Arrogant without proof |
| Street | Talks like the neighborhood, in on the joke | Forced slang, try-hard |
| Craft-obsessed | Specific about blades, guards, technique | Vague "premium experience" fluff |

### Copy rules

- Second person, present tense. Talk **at** the reader like a friend who won't lie to you.
- Sentences under 12 words hit harder. Fragments allowed. Encouraged.
- Prices are stated flat. No "starting at," no asterisks.
- ALL CAPS is a design element, not shouting — headlines and stamps only.
- Never: "elevate," "experience," "premium," "journey," "pamper," "gentlemen's grooming," emoji.

### Do / Don't copy examples

| Context | ✅ DO | ❌ DON'T |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Hero | "FRESH FADES. NO APPOINTMENTS NECESSARY. NO MERCY EITHER." | "Welcome to a premium grooming experience tailored to you." |
| Service | "Skin fade. Zero to whatever you're brave enough for. €30." | "Our signature skin fade service starts at just €30!" |
| Booking CTA | "BOOK A CHAIR" / "CLAIM YOUR SLOT" | "Schedule your appointment today!" |
| Walk-ins | "Walk-ins welcome. Cowards too." | "Walk-in availability may vary." |
| Testimonial intro | "DON'T TAKE OUR WORD FOR IT. TAKE THEIRS." | "What our valued clients are saying" |
| Error message | "That didn't work. Try again — we'll wait." | "Oops! Something went wrong. �servicedesk" |
| Hours | "OPEN TILL 8. SHARP TILL CLOSE." | "We look forward to serving you during business hours." |
| Confirmation | "LOCKED IN. Don't be late — the chair won't be." | "Thank you! Your booking has been received successfully." |

## 3. Color

Monochrome base + **one** aggressive accent. The accent is never decorative — it marks action, prices, and danger.

| Name | Hex | Role |
|------|-----|------|
| Asphalt | #0A0A0A | Page background (dark-first) |
| Concrete | #161616 | Raised surfaces, cards |
| Steel | #262626 | Borders, dividers |
| Smoke | #8A8A8A | Muted text |
| Bone | #F2F0EB | Primary text, inverted blocks (warm off-white, not pure white) |
| **Hazard Orange** | **#FF4D00** | CTAs, prices, hover states, ticker highlights, focus rings |
| Hazard Dim | #C93D00 | Pressed/active states |

Accessibility floor: Bone on Asphalt ≈ 17:1 (AAA). Hazard on Asphalt ≈ 5.9:1 (AA body, AAA large type). Black text on Hazard for filled buttons ≈ 5.6:1 (AA). Never place Hazard text on Concrete below 18px.

## 4. Typography

| Role | Font | Treatment |
|------|------|-----------|
| Display / headlines | **Anton** | ALL CAPS, tight tracking (-0.02em), huge (clamp 3rem → 12rem) |
| Body | **Space Grotesk** 400/500 | Sentence case, 1.6 line-height |
| Labels, prices, tickers, stamps | **JetBrains Mono** 500/700 | CAPS, letter-spacing 0.08em |

Typography carries the design. When in doubt: bigger headline, less decoration.

## 5. Imagery & Texture

- **Treatment (applies to every image):** high-contrast B&W, film grain overlay, slight crush in the blacks. Color arrives only via Hazard Orange duotone on hover/accent moments.
- **Motifs:** halftone dots, gaffer-tape strips, sticker/stamp badges, hazard stripes, slight off-grid rotation (−2° to 2°).
- **Never:** stock-photo smiles, purple/blue gradients, glassmorphism, soft drop shadows.

### AI image prompt base
```
Gritty editorial barbershop photography, high-contrast black and white,
35mm film grain, harsh single-source lighting, urban interior, tattooed
barber mid-cut, documentary style, crushed blacks, no smiling at camera
```

## 6. Logo / Wordmark

Type-set wordmark: "CUTTHROAT" in Anton, ALL CAPS, with a straight-razor slash replacing the crossbar geometry or a strike-through accent in Hazard Orange. Secondary stamp: circular "CBC — EST. 2019 — STAY SHARP" badge in JetBrains Mono, used as a rotating sticker element.

Don'ts: no gradients on the mark, no rounding, no outline-only version below 24px.
